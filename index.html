How to use it

Build a ledger you can trust, then look ahead

  1. 1

    Create a space

    A space is your ledger. Give it a name and a base currency. Keep Household and Studio in separate spaces so the numbers never mix.

  2. 2

    Add your accounts

    Chequing, savings, cash, credit — each account is an asset or a liability with an opening balance. Safe thresholds warn you before a day goes red.

  3. 3

    Log money on the day it hits

    Tap + and record income, spending, or a transfer. Transfers move money between accounts. They are never counted as income or spending.

  4. 4

    Plan what repeats

    Rent, paycheques, and subscriptions live as recurring plans. Occurrences stay estimated until you confirm or skip them. A plan is not a payment.

  5. 5

    Watch the forecast

    Home shows net position and the next 90 days. Calendar puts every dollar on its day. Insights totals the month you actually lived.

  6. 6

    Share on your terms

    Invite members by email, add split-only participants, and set balance policies. Roles cap what each person can see and change.

Questions

FAQ

What is a space?

A space is a shared ledger: accounts, categories, entries, and the people in it. You can keep more than one space — for example a household and a studio — and switch without mixing the numbers.

Does Daylance connect to my bank?

No. Daylance is a manual cash-flow planner. You log entries and opening balances yourself. It does not initiate payments or sync institution feeds.

How is a transfer different from income or spending?

A transfer moves money between your accounts. It can change an account balance without changing net worth, and it never counts as income or spending. Insights keep those lenses separate on purpose.

What happens to a recurring plan?

Recurring rules generate scheduled occurrences. They stay estimated until you confirm or skip them. Confirming posts the entry. Skipping leaves the forecast without pretending the money moved.

What's free and what's Daylance Pro?

Free includes one space and no added members. Daylance Pro is an auto-renewing subscription through the App Store that unlocks unlimited spaces and members. Pricing is shown in the app before you subscribe.

Do I need an account?

Yes. Sign in with Apple, Google, or email so your spaces can sync and, if you choose, be shared with members you invite.

Who can see a shared space?

Only people you invite as members, plus any split-only participants you add for balances. Roles cap what members can view and edit. Sharing is never implied by an ownership label alone.

Is this financial advice?

No. Forecasts are projections from the entries you log, not facts and not advice. Daylance is not a bank, broker, or tax product.

What happens to my data?

Your ledger lives in your account. Members of a space see what their role allows. We do not sell personal information. You can delete your account and data in the app. Sign-in stops immediately, and we remove the account and data after 30 days. You can also contact us from Contact. Read the full privacy policy.
