Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 18, 2026

Daylance ("we", "us", or "our") provides a calendar-led cash-flow planner. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices available to you.

What Daylance does

Daylance lets you create spaces, add financial accounts, log income, spending, and transfers, forecast balances, and optionally share a space with members you invite. Daylance does not connect to banks, move money, or provide financial, tax, or investment advice. Forecasts are projections from the entries you log, not facts.

Information we collect

  • Account information. When you sign in, we store identifiers needed to authenticate you and sync your data. That can include an email address, display name, and tokens from the sign-in method you choose (Apple, Google, or email and password).
  • Profile details. A display name and an optional profile photo you upload.
  • Ledger data. Spaces, accounts, categories, entries, notes, recurring plans, balance policies, opening balances, and other financial records you save. This is the core of the product.
  • Collaboration data. Member roles, invitations, and split-only participants you add. Invitation emails include the address you enter so we can deliver the invite.
  • Optional notifications. If you enable alerts, we store a device push token and the reminder preferences you set (for example, upcoming-entry digests).
  • Subscription status. If you subscribe to Daylance Pro, Apple and our payments provider tell us whether Pro is active so we can unlock plan limits. We do not receive your full payment card number.
  • Support requests. If you contact us, we receive the information you choose to send, such as your email address and request details.

We do not collect bank-login credentials. Daylance has no institution connection. We do not run a product-analytics pipeline that records merchant descriptions, notes, balances, or transaction amounts.

How we use information

  • Provide the ledger, forecasts, syncing, sharing, and reminders you ask for.
  • Authenticate your account and keep the service secure.
  • Store your spaces so they are available across your devices.
  • Send invitations and optional push notifications you enable.
  • Apply Daylance Pro entitlements after a store purchase or restore.
  • Respond to export, deletion, and other support requests.

How information is shared

We do not sell your personal information. We use service providers only as needed to run Daylance, such as:

  • Cloud hosting, authentication, database, and storage providers.
  • Sign-in providers you choose, such as Apple or Google.
  • Apple App Store and a subscription platform used to check Daylance Pro status.
  • Push-notification delivery for alerts you enable.
  • Email or support tooling used to send invitations and handle account requests.

These providers process data on our behalf under contractual and security obligations.

If you invite someone to a space, that member can see ledger information allowed by their role. Split-only participants are included in balances you configure; they do not get app access unless you also invite them as members.

Data retention

We keep account and ledger data while your account is active and as needed to provide the service. In the app, Delete account and data signs you out and blocks sign-in immediately. We keep the account and associated data for 30 days, then delete them unless we must retain limited information for legal, security, or dispute-resolution reasons.

Your choices and rights

  • Turn notification permissions off in the app or your device settings.
  • Leave a space, or ask a space owner to remove you.
  • Request an export of your account data by contacting us.
  • Delete your account and data in Settings or Profile. Sign-in stops immediately; we remove the account and data after 30 days.
  • Contact us at me@farnood.tech for help with privacy requests.

Security

We use technical and organizational measures designed to protect your information, including access controls, tenant isolation by space, and encrypted transport. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Children

Daylance is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Contact us if you believe a child has provided personal information.

International users

Daylance may process and store information in countries where we or our service providers operate. By using the app, you understand that your information may be transferred to those locations.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the effective date above. Material changes may also be communicated in the app or by other reasonable means.

Contact

For privacy questions, export requests, or deletion requests, use the contact page or email me@farnood.tech.
